# Reference motion spec: 10Web "Vibe for WordPress" launch film (Burnwe)

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=__0wj3bDeXo, 52.8 s, watched at 854x480 (YouTube's top quality in the browser pane).
Method: frames sampled every 0.5 s across the whole film, plus 15 fps strips through four transitions (1.6 to 2.6 s, 4.0 to 5.0 s, 19.9 to 20.9 s, 33.9 to 34.9 s). Audio was not analysed (muted sampling), so music sync points below are inferred from cut rhythm, not measured.

## Film-wide language
- Canvas: near-white lavender paper (#f4f3fb-ish) with a blue-violet light pool that lives along the bottom edge or one corner of almost every shot. That pool is the continuity device: it moves between shots instead of the camera cutting to a new world. Two dark interludes (6 to 8.5 s, 27.5 to 28.5 s) invert the paper to near-black with the same blue pool underneath.
- Type: one geometric grotesk (Manrope / Plus Jakarta-like), semibold, dark grey #2a2a2a, centred, roughly 6 to 8 % of frame height for statements, 15 to 20 % for single hero words ("Meet", "Vibe", "Code"). A soft grey drop shadow sits under the letters, so the text reads as an object on paper. Accent words switch to the blue-violet gradient (commas in "200,000,000", "Anymore", "Front-End", "Back-End", "Launched").
- Character: a glossy blue orb holding a four-point sparkle (the product mark). It opens the film, acts as the cursor/agent in the middle, gets zoomed through as a portal at 20 s and closes the film. The orb gives the film one object that carries across scenes.
- Pacing: 0 to 18 s is kinetic type, a new word group every 0.5 to 1.0 s, cut on the voice. 18 to 46 s is product, 2 to 4 s per shot with cursor actions. 46 to 52.8 s is social proof and logo, slower.
- Depth: depth-of-field blur on foreground orbs and avatar bubbles, 3D-tilted UI planes (about 20 to 30 degrees on Y, slight X), drop shadows under UI cards, a laptop render on fabric.
- Motion blur: strong on every whip pan, number roll and slash streak; none on settled type.

## Mechanics worth reusing (translated to our rules)
1. A persistent light pool or object that carries between shots, so cuts feel like one space. Our equivalent: the brand's thread or dot.
2. Pull-back reveal on a glyph: a hero glyph (the "/") arrives huge and becomes one character of a sentence that types out around it.
3. Odometer roll with per-digit stagger and vertical motion blur.
4. Zoom-through: the camera pushes into an object (the orb, the word "Code") until it fills frame and becomes the next shot's shape.
5. Shared-element expand: one tile in a grid grows into the full screen.
6. Frame break: UI cropped by the top edge with the caption below it on paper.
7. Staggered perspective stack of cards.
8. Long horizontal whip pan through one line of huge type with motion blur.
9. Kinetic word replacement at a fixed centre, cut on the voice.

## What we do not copy
- Their palette, orb, sparkle mark, glow and the purple gradient floor. The studio bans glow on UI and gradient-heavy looks; we take the mechanics, not the finish.
- Their centred-everything layout for every shot: we keep left-weighted compositions where the brand calls for it.
- Any of their footage, UI or 3D laptop render.
